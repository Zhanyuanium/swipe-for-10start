start-menu-legacy-swipe.wh.cpp: the shipping mod (0.27.0), `StartMenuExperienceHost.exe`. Raw HID decode from `RIDI_PREPARSEDDATA` + `HidP_GetUsageValue`: the report ID comes from the report's first byte when the device declares report IDs (`RAWINPUTHEADER::wParam` is only `RIM_INPUT`/`RIM_INPUTSINK`); a report whose ID carries no contact field at all is ignored as unrelated, distinct from `hid decodefail`; every candidate slot's `TipSwitch`/`InRange`/Contact Identifier (`0x0D/0x51`)/X/Y is decoded before a contact is chosen; the tracked contact is named by that identifier when both the device and the contact carry one (`identity=contactid`), else by `(device, node index)` (`identity=node`), so a slot the device hands to another finger does not transfer the drag; proximity to the last raw position only picks a new contact, and only that contact's declared tip-up or in-range clear ends it (a count-only lift report says nothing; the 18 ms idle ends the gesture with `src=idle`, and once no session owns the contact it clears the contact latch and forgets the identity); when several slots carry the tracked identifier the one that asserts contact wins, with one `hid contact id=... declared down in ... slots; using=...` line, while a coordinate-less report refreshes liveness only when it asserts contact — `TipSwitch`/`InRange`, or slot state Contact — never on identifier presence alone, and the liveness clock is separate from the position clock, so no position is fed; a device with neither tip nor in-range usage keeps the count/position path, logs `coordpath=1`, and applies its Contact Count and `(0,0)` rules in the tracked branch too, because it cannot assert a tip for the tracked slot; X/Y is scaled by the cap of the collection the point was read from (`cap.LinkCollection`). The dismiss axis and the panel band are probed from the taskbar itself — `SHAppBarMessage(ABM_GETTASKBARPOS)` first, the `Shell_TrayWnd`/`Shell_SecondaryTrayWnd` rectangle as the cross-check (flush to the edge and thin across it), the work-area inset only when it truly insets, never the Start window's rect; an inconclusive probe keeps the last axis and band, one line names what each source said, and the next open or broadcast retries. The band is the bar's strip extended by one bar thickness toward the desktop, and the axis is part of the layout identity, so an axis-only change bumps the token, drops the carried rect and republishes the trail. Axis-aware dismiss and reveal state machines on a worker thread drive `Progress` through one composition expression. One damped-spring settle serves all four gesture outcomes and the keyboard open; `AnimationStyle` picks only the damping — `Windows 11` (default) 1.72, fitted to the native Quick Settings flyout on this machine, and `Windows 10` 0.9 — and `GestureStartSpeedPercent` (default 100) scales the component of the finger's end speed toward the target: the widest eligible window of the last reports (8-30 ms), projected onto the settle direction and divided by the travel left, so a hold reports `vel=0.00`; 100 % is that component, and the curve's own end-value normalisation divides by its value at `T` — 0.99249 for `Windows 11` and 0.99589 for `Windows 10` at zero initial slope, so 1.007 and 1.004 are example seam factors, not constants — and the first keyframe, up to 4 ms in, shows the first segment's average speed. The commit test keeps the whole-gesture average and the 20% (10% on a vertical axis) travel; only the settle's seam reads the end. The duration is that travel at 2.2 DIP/ms clamped to 160-520 ms, and the curve is scaled by its own value at `T`, so the landing has no step. `TakeProgressNow()` is the only reader that turns the animation's recorded base — its target — into a position, and one close-ownership helper serves both close families: it reads the mute from the contact latch and either gesture family before either owner is cleared, so a held reveal is muted too, and the open publisher writes the cancelled kind with every new open generation so no `tapped` stamp survives into the next menu. Axis, `Distance`, the panel rect with its derived trail (the side facing away from the taskbar), the XAML origin, the work area, the monitor and the open generation are one `GestureGeometry` snapshot behind an `SRWLOCK` (the UI thread is the only writer and holds it exclusively for one struct assignment; a reader tries shared and answers its last whole copy on contention, except a new contact's first report, which waits for the writer when its cached copy has an obsolete identity and has no time bound if the writer is descheduled), and a snapshot from an obsolete layout is rejected for a new gesture; a layout change — open, `WM_DISPLAYCHANGE`, `SPI_SETWORKAREA` or `SizeChanged` — under a contact defers the whole turn (renderer scalars, panel and scroller) and one settled pass applies it after the release (`gesture geometry deferred reason=panel-owned gen=...`), so the edge, axis, travel and scroller rect never move under the finger; `g_dismissDistance` advances only after its scalar writes succeed. The apply reservation (`LayoutApplyGuard`) and the admission claim (`ClaimPanelForAdmission`) are `seq_cst`, so either the layout is deferred whole or that admission is refused and retried on the next report; a scalar write that throws after an earlier one landed marks the renderer invalid (`gesture renderer invalid until a successful reapply`) and refuses gestures until a full reapply. One idempotent measurement request per open (the layout watch's second pass or the 400 ms deadline) carries a request id and an epoch; its decision and its write are one critical section under `g_measureMutex`, the commit lock every epoch advance also takes, with all XAML measurement outside it; the panel publish re-checks id, epoch, axis and travel, and panel ownership at commit; the scroller commit re-checks id, epoch, layout version and panel ownership; and the previous rect is carried over only when that whole identity matches. The scroller cache is Unknown/Nothing/Scrollable: every remeasure publishes `Unknown` first, so a failed pass cannot leave a previous answer speaking, and the cached answer carries the layout version the gate checks beside the generation; only `Unknown` defers, `Nothing` is a completed negative search, an incomplete one stays `Unknown` and retries with 100/200/400 ms backoff, three attempts, logging `scroller retry why=... gen=... try=.../...` once per open and `scroller retry exhausted gen=...` when the budget is spent, and a positive candidate commits even when another branch was unreadable; a completed negative result revokes the old viewer watch. `PrepareRootElement` is gated on `StartUI_.dll` exactly 10.0.22621.3733 plus the prologue and its `E8` target, and both probes first require the inspected range to lie in an executable section of the loaded image (DOS/NT headers, `SizeOfImage`, section table), so `startui root hook skipped (target not readable)` is distinct from a build or signature mismatch; the `OnGlobalAnimationRequested` hook is installed on every load and passes through (`anim=1` default, `anim=0` drops the open request). The close record is three publishes of one seqlock record — plan, `tapped` immediately before the `VK_LWIN` tap, cancelled — and the open generation binds a bridge `retract` request to its menu. A close exists only once its settle starts: `BeginDismissSettle` answers not started, started, or started with a lost observer (`close observer lost kind=...`), and only a close that provably never started restores the panel, through `AbandonClose(kind)`, which is the whole operation because such a close published no plan and armed no tap. Unload is prerequisite-driven: the stop event exists before any thread starts (without it no raw-input thread is created and the mod logs `hid thread not started (stop event unavailable)`), the input thread watches it in its 10 ms message wait, the module-scan thread waits on the same event, and the 60 ms Windows tap waits on it and always releases the key. The image is pinned before any thread exists (`GetModuleHandleExW(GET_MODULE_HANDLE_EX_FLAG_FROM_ADDRESS | GET_MODULE_HANDLE_EX_FLAG_PIN)`, `mod pin image=... pinned=1`), because `void Wh_ModUninit` cannot veto the unload: only a successful pin keeps the image mapped until the process exits. The pin and the instance guard are prerequisites: either failing returns FALSE from `Wh_ModInit` with `mod init refused: ...; no hook, no channel and no thread installed`, and `g_initRefused` short-circuits the later lifecycle callbacks. One instance per process is taken with `Local\WindhawkLegacyStartSwipe.instance.<pid>` before the stop event, the channel or any thread; a second logs `instance guard refused: another instance is live in this process` and installs nothing, only a clean unload releases the mutex, and the name carries the PID, so it excludes another instance in that process, not every process in the session. A re-init of the same pinned image calls `ResetReinitState`, then `ResetActivationState` (`activation state reset`), which the input thread's exit runs too and which clears the tracked contact, the latch, both sessions, the close mute, the deferred flag, the pending tap and its serial, the pull serial, the coast, both velocity windows, the diagnostic latches and the `anim` override. Every entry into module code from the compositor or the dispatcher is counted from before it can run (`ModuleEntryLease`: the batch completion takes it before `batch.End()`, and every turn posted through `PostCountedUiTurn` takes it; the unload's revocation turn uses `RunAsync` plus an event and waits for it, deliberately not the counted post). A XAML revoke that throws keeps its token, and a new root refuses to overwrite an outstanding registration (`xaml size handler left attached; new registration refused`, same for `loaded`), so that root gets no renderer; the `SizeChanged` and `Loaded` callbacks are gated on the setup generation. Unload stops the module-scan thread (10 s wait), sets the event, waits 10 s for the input thread, revokes every XAML delegate on the UI thread, detaches the settle batch, drains `g_moduleEntryCount` to zero (2 s), then frees composition and the IPC view; a thread that misses its wait keeps its handle open, a joined one is closed, and any residual leaves every resource in place and logs `unload residual=...; process must restart to finish unloading`, and a stuck entry adds `unload entries outstanding=%d`. After a disable/enable without a host restart, the reused XAML root means no new `PrepareRootElement` and no renderer: a new contact is tracked but cannot drive the panel until the host restarts.
start-menu-touch-gesture-bridge.wh.cpp: `explorer.exe` bridge (0.13.0). Hooks `CStartExperienceManager::Show/Hide` (`Hide` is `HRESULT (void*, uint32_t)`, RVA `0x3C5960`). A Hide on an unconsumed `tapped` close record inside its 800 ms window is the Start side's own tap and runs at once (`hide passed (gesture)`), but only when the stamp is newer than the attach watermark and not older than the newest open tick it read; a plan, a cancelled record, a stale record or a second Hide under the same stamp is a keyboard close. A fresh stamp is correlation, not proof of cause: sequence and tick bind it to an open, but open and cancellation are separate records and equal ticks do not order them, so a Hide caused by the tap is inferred, not proven. That path publishes the `retract` request bound to the open generation it saw and a 700 ms deadline, waits up to 100 ms on the plan event for the answer, holds the original synchronously for that settle plus 30 ms (`hide delayed reason=... retract=... lead=...`), falls back to 560 ms for the sentinel or no answer, then marks the request done. No worker, manager pointer, timer or re-arm tap; every path calls the original. On unload it keeps new Hides out, waits up to 500 ms for in-flight `Hide` calls to drain, then closes the v2 channel (`ipc close`); the count is a `HideCallLease` (RAII, exactly one release per frame, taken before the hook reads anything), a Hide that outlives the drain keeps every handle and logs `ipc unload residual hides=...; the v2 channel stays until explorer exits`, and Windhawk 2.0.0-alpha.8 calls `Wh_ModUninit` before its bounded, result-discarding `ThreadsCallStackWaitForRegions(..., 200, 400)` and `FreeLibrary`, so that timeout is reachable and the residual is real; the bridge therefore keeps the handles and does not pin its image. The `start_gesture_shared.h` mapping and its event stay for the explorer process lifetime on purpose, because a consumer and a late gesture callback may still hold its view. Publishes the taskbar edge gesture handle and state from `twinui.pcshell.dll` into shared memory (not read by the shipping mod) and refuses to hook on a build mismatch.

start-menu-touch-gesture-probe.wh.cpp: Phase 0 probe that located `TouchEdgeGestureProcessor` and `ReportEdgeGesture` in `twinui.pcshell.dll`.
msgprobe.wh.cpp: diagnostic. Hooks `GetMessageW`/`PeekMessageW` and logs `WM_POINTER*` and mouse messages; established that the queue carries only ENTER + DOWN and then `WM_POINTERCAPTURECHANGED`.
inputprobe.wh.cpp: diagnostic. Raw HID listener plus a `CoreWindow`/`SystemUserAdapterWindowClass` WndProc subclass; it took the digitizer stream first and proved per-frame reports are readable. Must not run beside the shipping mod: Raw Input allows one sink per usage per process.
start_gesture_shared.h: the bridge's `Local\WindhawkStartGestureShared` contract (`StartGestureShared`, a seqlock over state, edge, gesture handle and points); read by the consumer probe, not by the shipping mod.
swipe_ipc_shared.h: the v2 channel contract shared by the Start mod, the bridge and `tools/ipc.ps1`: `SwipeIpcState` (672 bytes, every field pinned by an `offsetof` assert), one seqlock per record, the claim/ready init handshake, the close record with plan/tapped/cancel, the open generation and the retract request/plan pair. `SwipeIpcOpen` may wait up to the whole 550 ms budget, so a young claim is abandoned inside the call; `SwipeIpcReadOpen` refuses generation 0 with `ERROR_NOT_READY`; an `initState` outside the four defined values is refused at once with `ERROR_INVALID_DATA` instead of being waited on, and every retry path checks the deadline, so the budget holds for malformed state too; `tools/ipc.ps1` is the only command publisher and takes the mutex; a fresh stamp is correlation, not proof of cause.
