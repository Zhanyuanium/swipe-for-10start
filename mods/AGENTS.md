Method 7 (display change, lock screen, and the measured click-away, which all enter the same entry) is never held: a fresh reveal claim bound to the open on screen cancels it (`close cancelled method=7 ...`), anything else passes it at once (`close passed method=7 ...`), so the lock screen is never delayed and the click-away no longer animates. A fresh stamp is correlation, not proof of cause: sequence and tick bind it to an open, but open and cancellation are separate records and equal ticks do not order them, so a close caused by the tap is inferred, not proven. On unload it keeps new closes out and waits for every counted close-hook frame to leave before it closes the v2 channel; the count is a `CloseCallLease` (RAII, exactly one release per frame, over both hooks, taken before the hook reads anything) that spans the original call too, so zero is the only condition on which the drain closes the channel and returns: the wait has no deadline, zero logs `ipc unload drained waited=... ms`, closes the channel and logs `ipc close`, while a nonzero count logs `ipc unload drain over 500 ms closes=...` once and `ipc unload stalled closes=... waited=... ms` every 10 s — `stalled` is a periodic status, not an outcome, since the same unload can report it and then reach zero and log `drained`; the guarantee covers counted frames, because a thread already inside the detour but before its first increment belongs to the host hook-removal boundary.
